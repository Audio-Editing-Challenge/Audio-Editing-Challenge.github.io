## What is the registration deadline?

The registration deadline has been extended from October 1 to **October 8, 2026, at 11:59 PM U.S. Pacific Time**. Teams can register using the [registration form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSe3aLZSnqrpCq5Kg2Kw09Xvy0QpGZzraC9tzeGp-G6fob1q4g/viewform).

## What is the Agent Track model release cutoff?

The specific versions and weights of **all model components** in the agent system must have been publicly released **before November 1, 2026**. This extends the previous cutoff of October 1, 2026. See the [Agent Track rules](../track2/) for the full restrictions.

## Which data can teams use before submissions open?

**Before November 10, 2026**, participants may use the publicly available [MMAE test set](https://huggingface.co/datasets/BoJack/MMAE) to develop and evaluate their models and agent systems. **On November 10, 2026**, the organizers will release the previously unreleased challenge test set and the submission software development kit (SDK) for uploading results. The [leaderboard](../leaderboard/) will open for submissions on the same date.

## When is the final team member list confirmed?

Before submissions open on **November 10, 2026**, the organizers will send a form to collect and confirm each team's final member list, **including supervisors and team leaders**. Except in special circumstances, **changes to team membership will not be permitted once submissions open**.

## Can a person participate in both tracks or join multiple teams?

**Each person may participate in both tracks, but may be listed on only one team per track.** The confirmed member list includes supervisors and team leaders.
